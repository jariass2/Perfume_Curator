## 1. Personal Factors in Modern Perfume Recommendation Systems

### 1.1 Biological & Physical Factors

#### **Skin Type & pH Level**
- **Why it matters**: Skin chemistry significantly affects how fragrances develop
- **Categories used**:
  - Oily skin: Holds fragrance longer, may amplify sweet notes
  - Dry skin: Fragrance fades faster, needs moisturizing base notes
  - Normal/Combination: Most versatile
  - Sensitive: Requires hypoallergenic formulations
- **Advanced systems**: Some platforms ask about skin pH (acidic vs. alkaline) as it affects note projection

#### **Age**
- **18-25**: Preference for fresh, fruity, energetic scents (citrus, berries, light florals)
- **26-35**: Sophisticated florals, woody-fresh combinations, work-appropriate scents
- **36-50**: Complex compositions, oriental notes, confidence scents
- **50+**: Classic perfumes, powdery notes, elegant musks
- **Scientific basis**: Olfactory preferences evolve with hormonal changes and life experiences

#### **Body Temperature**
- Higher body temperature: Fragrances project more intensely (recommend lighter concentrations)
- Lower body temperature: May need stronger concentrations (EDP vs. EDT)

#### **Hormone Cycles** (for women)
- Some advanced systems account for monthly hormonal fluctuations
- Certain notes may smell different during different cycle phases

### 1.2 Environmental Factors

#### **Climate & Weather**
- **Hot/Humid**: Light citrus, aquatic, fresh notes (bergamot, sea salt, cucumber)
- **Cold/Dry**: Warm, rich, spicy notes (vanilla, amber, cinnamon, woods)
- **Temperate**: Versatile florals, green notes, balanced compositions
- **Seasonal variations**: Different recommendations for summer vs. winter

#### **Geographic Location**
- **Urban environments**: Cleaner, fresher scents (pollution consideration)
- **Coastal areas**: Aquatic, marine, ozonic notes
- **Rural/Nature**: Earthy, woody, green fragrances
- **Altitude**: High altitude may require stronger concentrations

#### **Air Quality**
- High pollution areas: Recommend scents that don't clash with urban smells
- Clean air environments: Can support more delicate, nuanced fragrances

### 1.3 Lifestyle Factors

#### **Professional Environment**
- **Corporate/Office**: Subtle, professional (soft musks, clean florals, light woods)
- **Creative Industries**: Bold, unique, artistic fragrances
- **Healthcare/Food Service**: Minimal or no fragrance policies
- **Client-facing**: Universally appealing, non-offensive scents
- **Work-from-home**: More freedom for experimental or stronger scents

#### **Activity Level**
- **High physical activity**: Sport fragrances, aquatic notes, high longevity
- **Sedentary lifestyle**: Wider range of options
- **Outdoor activities**: Nature-inspired, woody, fresh scents
- **Fitness enthusiasts**: Light, energizing citrus and mint combinations

#### **Social Life**
- **Frequent evening events**: Sophisticated evening fragrances (oud, amber, musk)
- **Daytime socializing**: Fresh, approachable scents
- **Dating/Romance**: Sensual notes (vanilla, jasmine, sandalwood)
- **Family-oriented**: Soft, comforting, universally pleasant

#### **Travel Frequency**
- Frequent travelers: Versatile day-to-night fragrances
- Recommendation of travel-sized options
- Multi-climate appropriate scents

### 1.4 Psychological & Personality Factors

#### **Personality Archetypes** (used by luxury brands)
- **The Sophisticate**: Classic, elegant, timeless (chypre, aldehydic florals)
- **The Adventurer**: Bold, exotic, unique (oud, incense, leather)
- **The Romantic**: Soft, feminine, floral (rose, peony, lily)
- **The Minimalist**: Clean, simple, transparent (white musk, clean cotton)
- **The Sensualist**: Warm, inviting, seductive (amber, vanilla, tonka)
- **The Naturalist**: Green, earthy, organic (vetiver, moss, fig)

#### **Emotional Goals**
- Confidence boost: Power scents (leather, oud, strong florals)
- Relaxation: Calming notes (lavender, chamomile, sandalwood)
- Energy: Invigorating citrus, mint, ginger
- Sensuality: Aphrodisiac notes (ylang-ylang, jasmine, musk)
- Focus: Rosemary, eucalyptus, peppermint

#### **Self-Expression**
- How visible/invisible they want to be
- Desire to stand out vs. blend in
- Cultural/personal identity expression

## 3. Advanced Matching Criteria Beyond Olfactory Families

### 3.1 Seasonality & Timing

#### **Seasonal Matching**
- **Spring**: Green, fresh, dewy (freesia, lily of the valley, cucumber)
- **Summer**: Citrus, aquatic, light florals (neroli, coconut, sea salt)
- **Autumn**: Spicy, warm, amber (cinnamon, cardamom, tonka bean)
- **Winter**: Rich, oriental, gourmand (vanilla, oud, chocolate, coffee)

#### **Time of Day**
- **Morning (6am-12pm)**: Energizing citrus, green tea, mint
- **Afternoon (12pm-6pm)**: Balanced florals, soft woods
- **Evening (6pm-12am)**: Rich orientals, ambers, musks
- **Night (12am-6am)**: Intense, intimate, deep bases

#### **Occasion-Based**
- **Work/Professional**: 3-5 hour longevity, moderate sillage
- **Casual Day**: 4-6 hours, light-moderate sillage
- **Evening Event**: 6-8 hours, strong sillage
- **Intimate/Date**: 4-6 hours, close-to-skin sillage
- **Special Occasion**: 8+ hours, bold projection

### 3.2 Longevity Preferences

#### **Concentration Understanding**
- **Eau Fraîche** (1-3%): 1-2 hours
- **Eau de Cologne** (3-5%): 2-3 hours
- **Eau de Toilette** (5-8%): 3-5 hours
- **Eau de Parfum** (8-15%): 5-8 hours
- **Parfum/Extrait** (15-30%): 8-24 hours

#### **User Preference Categories**
- **"Subtle Signature"**: Wants to reapply throughout day
- **"All-Day Companion"**: Single application for full day
- **"Performance Beast"**: Maximum longevity and projection
- **"Mood Shifter"**: Different scents throughout day

#### **Note Evolution Preferences**
- **Linear lovers**: Want consistent scent throughout wear
- **Evolution enthusiasts**: Enjoy dramatic top-to-base transitions
- **Base note focused**: Care most about dry-down

### 3.3 Sillage (Projection) Preferences

#### **Sillage Scale**
1. **Intimate (1-2 feet)**: "Skin scent", only noticeable when very close
2. **Moderate (2-4 feet)**: Noticeable in personal space
3. **Strong (4-8 feet)**: Announces presence in room
4. **Powerful (8+ feet)**: Room-filling, highly projecting

#### **User Profiles**
- **"Discreet Wearer"**: Prefers intimate sillage, personal enjoyment
- **"Confident Projector"**: Wants moderate-strong presence
- **"Statement Maker"**: Seeks powerful, attention-grabbing sillage
- **"Adaptable User"**: Different sillage for different occasions

#### **Cultural Considerations**
- **Western Europe/North America**: Generally moderate preference
- **Middle East**: Strong preference for powerful sillage
- **East Asia**: Often prefer intimate, subtle projection
- **Latin America**: Moderate to strong preference

### 3.4 Price Sensitivity & Value Perception

#### **Luxury Tiers**
- **Accessible Luxury** ($50-$100): Designer brands, larger bottles
- **Premium** ($100-$200): Niche brands, quality ingredients
- **High-End** ($200-$400): Exclusive niche, rare ingredients
- **Ultra-Luxury** ($400+): Artistic perfumery, limited editions

#### **Value Indicators Users Care About**
- Cost per wear (price ÷ estimated wears)
- ml per dollar
- Ingredient quality (natural vs. synthetic)
- Brand prestige
- Uniqueness/exclusivity
- Compliment factor

### 3.5 Ingredient Preferences & Restrictions

#### **Natural vs. Synthetic**
- **All-natural seekers**: Want botanical ingredients only
- **Synthetic-friendly**: Open to modern aromachemicals
- **Hybrid appreciators**: Best of both worlds

#### **Allergen Awareness**
- **Common allergens**: Oakmoss, linalool, limonene, citronellol
- **Hypoallergenic requests**: Increasing demand
- **Vegan/Cruelty-free**: Animal-derived musks, ambergris, castoreum

#### **Ingredient Transparency**

## 5. AI & Machine Learning Approaches to Fragrance Recommendations

### 5.1 Collaborative Filtering

#### **How it Works**
- Analyzes patterns: "Users who liked X also liked Y"
- Creates user clusters based on similar preferences
- Recommends based on cluster behavior

#### **Application to Perfumes**
- "Users who love Chanel No. 5 also enjoy Dior J'adore"
- Identifies hidden preference patterns
- Works best with large user bases

#### **Limitations**
- "Cold start problem" for new users
- May reinforce popular choices, miss niche preferences
- Needs significant data to be accurate

### 5.2 Content-Based Filtering

#### **How it Works**
- Analyzes perfume attributes (notes, family, concentration)
- Matches user preference profile to perfume characteristics
- Recommends similar items to liked products

#### **Application to Perfumes**
```
User likes: Bergamot, Rose, Sandalwood
System finds: Perfumes with similar note profiles
Weight by: Note prominence, concentration, reviews
```

#### **Advantages**
- Works for new users immediately
- Explains why recommendations made
- Discovers niche perfumes that match profile

### 5.3 Hybrid Recommendation Systems

#### **Combining Approaches**
- **Phase 1**: Content-based (initial recommendations)
- **Phase 2**: Collaborative filtering (refine based on behavior)
- **Phase 3**: Contextual (adjust for season, occasion, mood)

#### **Advanced Features**
- **Multi-armed bandit algorithms**: Balance exploration vs. exploitation
- **Deep learning**: Identify complex non-linear preference patterns
- **Natural Language Processing**: Analyze review sentiment

### 5.4 Olfactory Note Embedding

#### **Concept**
- Represent fragrance notes as vectors in multi-dimensional space
- Notes close in space are similar (e.g., "vanilla" near "tonka bean")
- Calculate perfume similarity by vector distance

#### **Example Implementation**
```
Rose vector: [floral: 0.9, sweet: 0.6, powdery: 0.4, fresh: 0.3]
Jasmine vector: [floral: 0.9, sweet: 0.5, powdery: 0.2, fresh: 0.6]

Distance = sqrt(sum((Rose[i] - Jasmine[i])^2))
```

#### **Applications**
- Find "similar but different" recommendations
- Identify substitute perfumes
- Create scent journey progressions

### 5.5 Sentiment Analysis on Reviews

#### **Text Mining Approaches**
- Analyze thousands of fragrance reviews
- Extract common descriptors per perfume
- Identify emotional associations

#### **Example Insights**
```

- "fresh" (87% positive)
- "masculine" (92% positive)
- "compliment getter" (79% positive)
- "office appropriate" (64% positive)
```

#### **Application**
- Match user goals to review sentiments
- Identify "hidden features" not in official descriptions
- Predict user satisfaction likelihood

### 5.6 Computer Vision for Bottle Aesthetics

#### **Note Matching Sub-Algorithm**
```python
desired_notes_present = count(user.desired_notes ∩ perfume.notes)
avoided_notes_absent = count(user.avoided_notes ∩ perfume.notes) == 0

note_score = (
    (desired_notes_present / len(user.desired_notes)) * 0.7 +
    (avoided_notes_absent ? 1 : 0) * 0.3
)
```

### 7.2 Cosine Similarity for Perfume Matching

#### **Concept**
Treat perfumes as vectors in multi-dimensional space where each dimension is a note or attribute.

#### **Implementation**
```python
# User preference vector
user_vector = [
    citrus_love: 0.9,
    floral_love: 0.3,
    woody_love: 0.6,
    sweet_love: 0.4,
    spicy_love: 0.1
]

# Perfume A profile
perfume_a_vector = [
    citrus: 0.8,
    floral: 0.2,
    woody: 0.7,
    sweet: 0.3,
    spicy: 0.1
]

# Cosine similarity
similarity = dot_product(user_vector, perfume_a_vector) /
             (magnitude(user_vector) * magnitude(perfume_a_vector))

# Result: 0.98 (excellent match)
```

        olfactory_match * 0.20 +      # Core preference
        note_match * 0.20 +            # Specific notes
        intensity_match * 0.10 +       # Usage context
        gender_match * 0.05 +          # Basic filter
        climate_match * 0.10 +         # NEW: Environmental fit
        season_match * 0.08 +          # NEW: Temporal appropriateness
        longevity_match * 0.10 +       # NEW: Performance expectation
        sillage_match * 0.10 +         # NEW: Projection preference
        skin_match * 0.05 +            # NEW: Biological fit
        price_match * 0.02             # NEW: Budget consideration
    )

    return round(compatibility * 100, 1)  # Return as percentage


def calculate_climate_fit(user_climate, perfume_climate, notes):
    """
    Match user's climate with perfume's optimal climate
    """
    climate_note_mapping = {
        'Cálido': ['citrus', 'aquatic', 'green', 'marine'],
        'Frío': ['amber', 'vanilla', 'spicy', 'woody'],
        'Templado': ['floral', 'fruity', 'soft_woody']
    }

    # Direct climate match
    if user_climate == perfume_climate:
        return 1.0

    # Check if perfume notes suit user's climate
    suitable_notes = climate_note_mapping.get(user_climate, [])
    note_overlap = len(set(notes) & set(suitable_notes)) / len(suitable_notes)

    return max(0.6, note_overlap)  # Minimum 60% match


def calculate_season_fit(user_season, perfume_seasonal_rating):
    """
    Seasonal appropriateness scoring
    perfume_seasonal_rating = {'Primavera': 0.8, 'Verano': 0.9, 'Otoño': 0.4, 'Invierno': 0.3}
    """
    if user_season == 'Todo el año':
        # For all-season request, check if perfume is versatile
        avg_rating = sum(perfume_seasonal_rating.values()) / 4
        return avg_rating

    return perfume_seasonal_rating.get(user_season, 0.5)


def match_longevity_expectation(desired_hours, perfume_avg_hours):
    """
    Score based on how well perfume longevity matches expectation
    """
    hour_ranges = {
        '2-4 horas': (2, 4),
        '4-6 horas': (4, 6),
        '6-8 horas': (6, 8),
        'Todo el día (8+)': (8, 24)
    }

    min_desired, max_desired = hour_ranges[desired_hours]

    if min_desired <= perfume_avg_hours <= max_desired:
        return 1.0
    elif perfume_avg_hours < min_desired:
        # Too short - penalty proportional to shortage
        return max(0.3, perfume_avg_hours / min_desired)
    else:
        # Too long - minor penalty (longer is often better)
        return max(0.7, max_desired / perfume_avg_hours)


def calculate_skin_compatibility(skin_type, notes):
    """
    Adjust based on how notes interact with skin type
    """
    adjustments = {
        'Grasa': {
            'amplifies': ['sweet', 'vanilla', 'gourmand'],  # May be overwhelming
            'moderates': ['fresh', 'citrus', 'aquatic']     # Good fit
        },
        'Seca': {
            'fades_quickly': ['citrus', 'aquatic', 'light_florals'],
            'benefits_from': ['amber', 'woody', 'rich_florals']
        },
        'Normal/Mixta': {
            'versatile': True  # No major adjustments needed
        }
    }

    if skin_type == 'Normal/Mixta':
        return 1.0

    skin_data = adjustments.get(skin_type, {})
    note_categories = categorize_notes(notes)

    # Check for problematic combinations
    if 'amplifies' in skin_data:
        amplified_present = any(cat in note_categories for cat in skin_data['amplifies'])
        if amplified_present:
            return 0.85  # Slight penalty, might be too strong

    if 'fades_quickly' in skin_data:
        fading_present = any(cat in note_categories for cat in skin_data['fades_quickly'])
        if fading_present:
            return 0.80  # Penalty for poor longevity on this skin type

    if 'benefits_from' in skin_data:
        beneficial = any(cat in note_categories for cat in skin_data['benefits_from'])
        if beneficial:
            return 1.1  # Bonus for ideal match

    return 1.0

## 9. Competitive Analysis - Leading Platforms

### 9.1 Fragrantica (Information Database)
**Strengths**:
- Massive user-generated database
- Detailed note pyramids
- User reviews with voting system
- "This reminds me of" feature
- Seasonal/occasion tags

**Recommendation Method**:
- Primarily user-driven discovery
- "Similar fragrances" based on note overlap
- No personalized quiz

**Lesson for Paymsa**: User reviews and "similar perfumes" features add value

### 9.2 Scentbird (Subscription Service)
**Strengths**:
- Best-in-class personalization quiz
- Monthly discovery model
- Detailed preference tracking over time
- "Skip this month" flexibility

**Recommendation Method**:
- 7-question visual quiz
- Collaborative filtering (what similar users like)
- Continuous learning from ratings

**Lesson for Paymsa**: Progressive profiling through monthly feedback is powerful

### 9.3 Hawthorne (Men's Personalized)
**Strengths**:
- Lifestyle-focused quiz (work environment, hobbies)
- Creates custom blends
- Personality-based matching

**Recommendation Method**:
- 20+ question detailed quiz
- Proprietary algorithm considering lifestyle
- A/B testing different formulations

**Lesson for Paymsa**: Lifestyle context matters significantly for men's fragrances

### 9.4 Olfactif (Artisan Discovery)
**Strengths**:
- Curated artisan/niche focus
- Educational approach
- Story-driven presentation

**Recommendation Method**:
- Quiz focuses on emotional response
- Curator-selected matches
- Hybrid human + algorithm

**Lesson for Paymsa**: Luxury positioning benefits from storytelling + curation

### 9.5 Phlur (Clean Fragrance)
**Strengths**:
- Sustainability focus
- "Try before you buy" samples
- Community-driven

**Recommendation Method**:
- Simple 3-question start
- Visual preference mapping
- Emphasizes ingredient transparency

**Lesson for Paymsa**: Transparency and values alignment matter to luxury consumers
